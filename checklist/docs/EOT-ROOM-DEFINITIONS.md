# End of Tenancy (EOT) Room Definitions

**Status**: Specification Complete  
**Purpose**: Define all room types and variants for end-of-tenancy deep cleaning  
**Classification**: Single (Deep Clean Only - EOT is always thorough)

---

## Overview

End of Tenancy cleaning is the most comprehensive residential cleaning service. It's a **single classification** (always deep) because:
- Legal requirement: Property must be spotless for landlord inspection
- Turnover deadline: Next tenant moving in (no room for shortcuts)
- All extras required: Inside appliances, behind furniture, top shelves, windows

**Key Characteristic**: EOT is the **most intensive** cleaning profile. Everything gets deep attention.

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Legal compliance deep clean |
| **Scope** | 100% thorough (inside appliances, behind furniture, ceilings) |
| **Typical Size** | 2-4 bedroom homes, 600-2000+ sq ft |
| **Production Rate** | 330-380 sq ft/hr (slowest of all types) |
| **Difficulty** | Majority medium-hard items, some specialty items |

---

## Room Categories & Variants

EOT cleaning covers similar room types to residential, but with **extra detail items** built-in. Every room has ceiling decobing, baseboards detail, inside appliances, etc.

### Category 1: BEDROOMS (5 variants)

Bedrooms in EOT include: ceiling decobing, baseboard wipe-down, window sills, wardrobe organization.

#### Bedroom Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_bed_single` | Single Bedroom | Small room, single bed, minimal furniture | 80-120 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_bed_master` | Master Bedroom | Large room, king/queen bed, walk-in wardrobe | 200-300 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_bed_guest` | Guest Bedroom | Medium room, queen/double bed, basic furniture | 120-180 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_bed_bunks` | Bunk Bedroom (Kids) | Multiple bunk beds, under-bed storage | 100-150 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_bed_loft` | Loft Bedroom | High ceiling, mezzanine, extra storage | 150-250 sq ft | hard |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- Ceiling decobing/cobweb removal (all bedrooms)
- Baseboards: detail wipe-down, remove marks
- Light switches and covers: wipe and polish
- Door frames: wipe interior and exterior
- Wardrobe/closet: wipe shelves, remove dust, organize
- Under-bed areas: vacuum, wipe baseboards
- Window sills: detailed clean, condensation removal
- Window treatments: dust blinds or curtains

**Difficulty Notes**:
- Master bedroom = hard (larger space, more items to detail)
- Loft bedroom = hard (ceiling height work, safety concerns)
- Single/bunks = medium (smaller, faster)

### Category 2: BATHROOMS (6 variants)

Bathrooms in EOT include: grout cleaning, mirror polishing, inside cabinet cleaning, exhaust vent cleaning.

#### Bathroom Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_bath_single_shower` | Single Shower | Single toilet, shower only, single sink | 35-45 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_bath_single_tub` | Single Bathtub | Single toilet, bathtub only, single sink | 35-45 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_bath_single_combo` | Single Combo | Single toilet, shower+tub, single sink | 40-50 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_bath_ensuite` | Ensuite Shower | Off-bedroom, shower only, single sink | 25-35 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_bath_ensuite_tub` | Ensuite Bathtub | Off-bedroom, bathtub only, single sink | 25-35 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_bath_double_combo` | Double Bathroom | Dual toilets possible, shower+tub, double sink, heated rails | 50-70 sq ft | hard |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- Tiles and grout: Scrub grout lines (toothbrush if needed)
- Mirrors and glass: Polish, remove water spots, steam residue
- Toilet bowl: Deep clean, descale, scrub under rim
- Tub/shower: Scrub surround, remove soap scum, descale
- Sink: Polish, remove hard water stains, clean overflow
- Cabinets: Wipe interior and exterior, remove items temporarily if needed
- Exhaust vent: Clean grille, remove dust buildup
- Baseboards: Detail wipe
- Light switches: Polish
- Door: Clean interior and exterior
- Heated towel rails: Descale if mineral buildup
- Caulking: Wipe, ensure seal intact

**Difficulty Notes**:
- Bathtub = harder (tub scrubbing is labor-intensive)
- Combo (shower+tub) = hardest (double the fixtures)
- Double bathroom = hardest (double the work, double fixtures)
- Ensuite = easiest (compact, no windows usually)

### Category 3: KITCHENS (4 variants)

Kitchens in EOT include: inside oven/fridge, cabinet cleaning, range hood filter, window/sill detail.

#### Kitchen Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_kitchen_small` | Small/Galley | Narrow layout, basic appliances | 80-120 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_kitchen_standard` | Standard Kitchen | Medium L-shaped, typical appliances | 120-160 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_kitchen_island` | Island Kitchen | Island bench, larger counter space, multiple appliances | 160-220 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_kitchen_modern_open` | Modern Open-Plan | Open to lounge, high-end finishes, large island | 180-280 sq ft | hard |

**All kitchens in EOT are "hard"** because appliance detail is mandatory.

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- **Oven**: Inside clean (if accessible without professional equipment)
  - Remove racks, soak and scrub
  - Wipe interior, remove burnt-on food
  - Replace racks and clean
  - Polish exterior
- **Fridge**: 
  - Remove shelves and drawers
  - Wash and dry
  - Wipe interior walls
  - Clean coils (if accessible)
  - Replace shelves/drawers
- **Microwave**: 
  - Remove turntable, wash and dry
  - Wipe interior, remove food splatters
  - Clean exterior, vents
- **Stove/Cooktop**: 
  - Remove burner covers, soak and scrub
  - Wipe down cooktop surface
  - Clean around burners
- **Cabinets**: 
  - Wipe interior and exterior
  - Remove any items left behind
  - Wipe shelves
- **Range hood**: 
  - Remove and wash/replace filter
  - Wipe exterior and ductwork (if accessible)
- **Dishwasher**: 
  - Empty racks
  - Wipe interior, seals
  - Run empty cycle if needed
- **Benchtops**: 
  - Stone: Clean with pH-neutral cleaner
  - Timber: Oil or wax as needed
  - Laminate: Careful with moisture
- **Splashback**: 
  - Tile: Grout cleaning
  - Glass/stainless: Polish
- **Baseboards**: Detail wipe
- **Walls**: Wipe marks, cooking splatters
- **Windows/Sills**: Polish, remove condensation, detail frames
- **Door and handles**: Polish inside and outside
- **Light fixtures**: Dust and wipe

**Kitchen Hours**: Generally 2-4 hours depending on size and appliance complexity.

### Category 4: LAUNDRY (3 variants)

Laundry spaces in EOT include: washer/dryer exterior deep clean, shelving organization, tub scrubbing.

#### Laundry Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_laundry_basic` | Basic Laundry | Compact space, no major equipment | 30-40 sq ft | basic |
| `eot_laundry_with_tub` | Laundry with Tub | Separate tub, shelving | 40-60 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_laundry_with_washer` | Laundry with Washer | Washer, shelving, sink | 50-80 sq ft | medium |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- Washer/dryer: 
  - Wipe exterior (inside lint traps, door seals)
  - Remove any items left inside
- Tub: 
  - Scrub thoroughly
  - Remove drain cover and clean
  - Wipe skirting
- Shelves: 
  - Remove items, wipe shelves
  - Organize or remove items
  - Wipe edge and undersides
- Walls: Wipe, remove marks
- Floor: Vacuum and mop thoroughly
- Baseboards: Detail wipe
- Door/frame: Polish

### Category 5: LIVING AREAS (4 variants)

Living spaces in EOT include: ceiling detail, light fixture cleaning, skirting board detail, window/blind cleaning.

#### Living Area Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_living_lounge` | Lounge/Living Room | Seating area, TV area | 120-180 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_living_dining` | Dining Room | Dining table area, possibly sideboard | 100-150 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_living_family` | Family Room | Multi-purpose, larger | 150-220 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_living_entryway` | Entryway/Foyer | Entry hall, shoe storage | 40-80 sq ft | medium |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- Ceiling: Decobing, light fixture dusting
- Walls: Wipe marks, scuffs, clean around light switches
- Floor: Vacuum thoroughly, mop hardwood or sweep tiles
- Baseboards: Detail wipe along entire room
- Windows: Detail clean, polish frames, sills
- Blinds/curtains: Dust or wipe (don't usually launder in EOT)
- Light fixtures: Dust, wipe, ensure working
- Door: Polish inside and outside
- Closets/storage: Remove items, wipe shelves, organize

### Category 6: UTILITY & SPECIAL ROOMS (4 variants)

Special spaces often found in EOT properties.

#### Utility Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Size | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_storage` | Storage/Pantry | Pantry shelving, storage areas | 40-80 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_office` | Home Office | Desk area, shelving, computer space | 60-100 sq ft | medium |
| `eot_garage` | Garage | Attached or separate garage | 200-400 sq ft | hard |
| `eot_utility_closet` | Utility/Laundry Closet | Compact laundry/storage | 30-50 sq ft | medium |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- **Storage/Pantry**:
  - Remove items from shelves
  - Wash and dry items or remove them
  - Wipe shelves thoroughly
  - Organize or dispose of items left behind
- **Office**:
  - Dust all surfaces
  - Wipe desk
  - Remove dust from computer equipment (carefully)
  - Wipe baseboards and ceiling
  - Detail window cleaning
- **Garage**:
  - Sweep floor thoroughly
  - Mop if concrete
  - Wipe walls for grease/marks
  - Remove any items left behind
  - Wipe door and handles
  - Clean garage door interior (if accessible)
  - Oil or rust check if applicable
- **Utility Closet**:
  - Remove items
  - Wipe shelves and walls
  - Clean door interior/exterior

### Category 7: WINDOWS & EXTERNAL (2 variants)

External areas in EOT.

#### External Variants

| Variant ID | Name | Description | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| `eot_windows` | Windows (All) | Interior and exterior window cleaning | medium |
| `eot_deck_patio` | Deck/Patio/Balcony | External floor areas | medium |

**EOT-Specific Items**:
- **Windows**:
  - Interior glass: Polish, remove condensation
  - Exterior glass: Polish, remove weather marks
  - Frames: Detail wipe interior and exterior
  - Sills: Remove dirt, clean grooves
  - Blinds/tracks: Wipe or vacuum
  - Seals/caulking: Check for integrity
- **Decks/Patios**:
  - Sweep thoroughly
  - Mop or pressure wash if available
  - Remove any debris
  - Check for stains or damage

## EOT Room Composition

### Mandatory Rooms (must have at least one of each)

1. At least 2 bedrooms (most EOT rentals are 2+ BR)
2. At least 1 bathroom (can be single or combo)
3. Kitchen (any size)
4. Living area (lounge or dining or both)
5. Entryway/hallway

### Always Included

- Windows (every room has window items)
- Baseboards (entire property)
- Ceiling decobing (entire property)
- Light switches and fixtures (entire property)

### Optional Add-ons

- Extra bedrooms
- Extra bathrooms
- Laundry room
- Office/storage
- Garage
- Outdoor areas (deck, patio)

## Comparison: EOT vs Residential vs Commercial

| Aspect | EOT | Residential (Light) | Residential (Deep) | Commercial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inside Appliances** | ✅ Yes (mandatory) | ❌ No (light) | ✅ Yes (optional) | ❌ No |
| **Ceiling Decobing** | ✅ Yes (every room) | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes (commercial) |
| **Baseboards Detail** | ✅ Yes (entire home) | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (touchpoints) |
| **Window Exteriors** | ✅ Yes (all) | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| **Grout Cleaning** | ✅ Yes (bathrooms) | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| **Consumables Restocked** | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | ✅ Yes (commercial) |
| **Production Rate** | 330-380 sq ft/hr | 400-500 sq ft/hr | 360-400 sq ft/hr | 330-400 sq ft/hr |
| **Typical Duration** | 3-8 hours | 2-4 hours | 4-8 hours | Varies (hourly) |
