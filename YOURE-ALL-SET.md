## 🎯 The Spinning Button Explained

When you click "🔄 Regenerate Checklist":

```
Click Button
    ↓
Icon spins 360° (smooth animation)
    ↓
Text says "Regenerating..."
    ↓
Button disabled (no double-clicks)
    ↓
generateChecklistFromForms() runs
    ↓
Checklist updates with new config
    ↓
After 600ms animation:
Icon resets + text resets + button re-enabled
```

**Result:** Clear visual feedback that the click was captured and processed. ✅
